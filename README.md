# 3.11 Spectroscopy and the Electromagnetic Spectrum

Interactive simulation for AP Chemistry Topic 3.11 (Learning Objective 3.11.A).
Students relate each region of the electromagnetic spectrum to the molecular or
electronic transition it causes: microwave to rotation, infrared to vibration,
ultraviolet/visible to electronic transitions.

**Live:** https://mauiscience.github.io/lava-lab-science/3-11-spectroscopy/

## What's inside
- **Explore**
  - Spectrum and molecular motion: drag a wavelength marker and watch H2O, CO2,
    or HCl respond, with a matching energy-level diagram.
  - Infrared: nine compounds, a bond-absorption table, and a bond vibration
    animation.
  - UV-Vis: a spectrophotometer with two cuvettes, absorbance vs. wavelength,
    and absorbance vs. concentration.
- **Guided Lab:** three investigations with check questions, including
  unknown IR spectra and an unknown-concentration match.
- **Quiz:** three tiers, scored Mastery / Proficient / Review.

## Notes
- Single self-contained HTML file. No login, no data collection, no external
  dependencies. Works offline.
- Spectra are simplified. Peak positions follow typical correlation ranges.
- All content and graphics are original (SVG/Canvas, no third-party assets).
