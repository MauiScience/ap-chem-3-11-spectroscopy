What the radiation does to a molecule

Energy levels

The wave is not drawn to scale. A real molecule absorbs only photons whose energy matches the gap between two of its levels; the region tells you which kind of gap that is.

Infrared spectra

Move over the spectrum to read a wavenumber.

Which bond absorbs where?

Rows light up for the wavenumber under your cursor.

Why the positions differ

Each bond is slowed down by a factor of about 1012 so you can see it. Lighter atoms and stronger (multiple) bonds vibrate faster, so they absorb at higher wavenumber.

Band positions follow typical correlation ranges; peak shapes are simplified and the real spectra have more small bands.
